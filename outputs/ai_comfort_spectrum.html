Positions were read off photographs of each table: the leftmost card on a table’s tape is 1 and the rightmost is 10, with everything between interpolated along the tape. Cards sitting within 0.3 of each other share a number. Measured is the unrounded position before that rounding — it is why two cards can read 5 from 5.35 and 5.60. Three photographs were set aside before numbering: one table was withdrawn, one was framed too tightly to anchor its scale, and one was a second shot of a table already counted.
